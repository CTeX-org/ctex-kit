\begin{center}\Large issue \#1069 MWE\end{center}

\noindent 报告者的原始样例：

\medskip
\noindent\verb|\pinyin{nv3}| \quad→\quad \pinyin{nv3}

\medskip
\noindent\verb|\pinyin{nü3}| \quad→\quad \pinyin{nü3}

\bigskip
\noindent 更多等价写法对照：

\medskip
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\verb|\pinyin{lv2zi}|     & \pinyin{lv2zi}     & \verb|\pinyin{lü2zi}|     & \pinyin{lü2zi} \\
\verb|\pinyin{nv3hai2zi}| & \pinyin{nv3hai2zi} & \verb|\pinyin{nü3hai2zi}| & \pinyin{nü3hai2zi} \\
\verb|\pinyin{jv4}|       & \pinyin{jv4}       & \verb|\pinyin{jü4}|       & \pinyin{jü4} \\
\verb|\pinyin{Nv3}|       & \pinyin{Nv3}       & \verb|\pinyin{Nü3}|       & \pinyin{Nü3} \\
\verb|\pinyin{nv}|        & \pinyin{nv}        & \verb|\pinyin{nü}|        & \pinyin{nü} \\
\end{tabular}

\bigskip
\noindent 注音命令同样接受：\quad
\verb|\xpinyin{女}{nü3}| → \xpinyin{女}{nü3}
